# V17 — Intelligent Campaign Manager

## Purpose
Move from isolated posts to coherent multi-day Christian content series without weakening the existing biblical and safety controls.

## Campaign behavior
The manager produces 3–14 episode briefs around a central theme. It rotates formats, avoids immediate topic repetition, and carries the Growth Brain's pillar and hook direction into each episode.

## Strategy modes
- EXPLORE: sparse data; prioritize learning and variety.
- BALANCED: combine proven signals with controlled exploration.
- EXPLOIT: stronger evidence exists; emphasize proven pillars while retaining variation.

These modes are heuristics, not guarantees of reach or virality.

## Non-negotiables
Every episode remains subject to Scripture verification, biblical consistency, Gospel clarity, safety, platform validation, renderer validation and publishing idempotency. The campaign manager cannot override those gates.
