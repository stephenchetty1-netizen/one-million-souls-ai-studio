# V54 — Autonomous Pastoral Wisdom & Audience Care

## Purpose

V54 adds a dedicated pastoral-responsibility review between Christian ethics and independent theological consistency. The goal is not to make the AI a pastor. The goal is to prevent technically correct Christian content from becoming careless, coercive, age-inappropriate, or falsely individualized.

## Review dimensions

1. Audience care
2. Tone and compassion
3. Age appropriateness
4. Harm and vulnerability
5. Practical wisdom
6. Pastoral boundaries
7. Next step and support

## Guardrails

- Spiritual encouragement is not a substitute for qualified professional care.
- The system does not diagnose mental-health conditions, trauma, abuse, addiction, or spiritual states.
- It does not promise healing, emotional, financial, or life outcomes from prayer or obedience.
- It does not encourage secrecy from safe adults, guardians, pastors, or professionals when safeguarding may matter.
- It avoids shame, coercion, fear, humiliation, and pressure to disclose private information.
- It uses age-appropriate language and avoids unnecessary adult themes or unsafe instructions.
- Material uncertainty or harmful patterns fail closed.

## Integration

The orchestrator now runs:

`Apologetics → Christian Ethics → Pastoral Wisdom → Theological Consistency → Production`

The V40 theological consistency prompt is also upgraded to receive downstream ethics and pastoral-wisdom evidence, so the final independent gate can challenge and reconcile those reviews rather than silently ignoring them.
