# V28 — Autonomous Learning & Decision Agent

## Purpose
Turn measured public performance signals into the next content decision while preserving biblical and platform guardrails.

## Decision modes
- INSUFFICIENT_DATA: do not make a major change.
- EXPLORE: recent underperformance supports testing a different hook/format.
- EXPLOIT: repeat winning patterns with one controlled variation.
- BALANCED: preserve strategy and introduce a modest variation.

## Loop
POST-PUBLISH INTELLIGENCE -> DECISION AGENT -> ORCHESTRATOR -> GENERATE -> PRODUCE -> PUBLISH -> ANALYTICS

## Guardrails
No fabricated Scripture, deceptive promises, fabricated testimony, private audience data collection, or unlicensed media. One major creative variable is changed at a time when evidence is limited.
