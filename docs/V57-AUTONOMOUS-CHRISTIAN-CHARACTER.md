# V57 — Autonomous Christian Character & Virtue Review

## Purpose
Audit Christian content for faithful encouragement toward Christlike character without turning virtue into a means of earning salvation, acceptance, or platform status.

## Review dimensions
- Love & compassion
- Humility & servanthood
- Integrity & truthfulness
- Patience & self-control
- Courage & faithfulness
- Mercy & forgiveness
- Christlike motivation
- Grace & works boundary

## Safety and theological guardrails
The reviewer distinguishes biblical virtues from personality preferences, avoids inferring a viewer's heart or salvation, rejects shame and coercion, avoids promises of earthly outcomes, and preserves wise boundaries around forgiveness, abuse, safeguarding, and individualized pastoral claims.

## Orchestration
`COMMUNITY → CHARACTER → THEOLOGICAL CONSISTENCY → FACTORY → MEDIA → VIDEO PLAN → ASSETS → RENDER → PUBLISH`
