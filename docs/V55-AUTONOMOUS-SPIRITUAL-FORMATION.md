# V55 — Autonomous Spiritual Formation & Prayer Review

V55 adds an independent review layer for Christian prayer and spiritual-formation content.

## Purpose

Ensure that content encourages faithful prayer and discipleship without turning spiritual practices into formulas, transactions, guarantees, or substitutes for Christ, Scripture, Christian community, wisdom, or appropriate support.

## Review dimensions

- Prayer faithfulness
- Discipleship practices
- Scripture/practice alignment
- Grace and dependence on Christ
- Spiritual growth boundaries
- Community and church
- Motivation and heart

## Guardrails

The reviewer blocks or requires revision for prayer-as-formula, guaranteed outcomes, works-based acceptance with God, transactional spirituality, fabricated testimonies, engagement-as-spiritual-growth, spiritual diagnoses, and claims that one practice replaces Scripture or Christian community.

## Integration

The orchestrator runs this layer after Pastoral Wisdom and before final Theological Consistency. Publication remains fail-closed if the review is invalid.
