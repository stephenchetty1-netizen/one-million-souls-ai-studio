# V15 — Creative Intelligence Engine

## Purpose
V15 turns performance learning into a repeatable creative decision. Instead of generating content from topic alone, the system chooses a presentation direction while keeping Scripture authoritative.

## Decision inputs
- Recent performance records
- Winning hooks and topics
- Saved growth strategy
- Aggregate audience recommendations
- Active experiments
- Amount of available data

## Creative outputs
- Hook style
- Story style
- Scripture presentation
- Visual style
- CTA style
- Platform priority
- Confidence and exploration mode
- Evidence summary and guardrails

## Safety and truth hierarchy
1. Verified Scripture and biblical context
2. Biblical/safety quality gate
3. Platform compliance
4. Creative intelligence
5. Performance optimization

Creative performance can never justify changing or inventing biblical claims. The engine also avoids guaranteed-outcome language, deceptive fear tactics, and fabricated testimony.

## Production loop
Growth Brain → Creative Intelligence → Bible Research → Writer → Quality Gate → Content Factory → Renderer → Metricool → Analytics → Learning.
