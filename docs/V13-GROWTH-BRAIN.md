# V13 — Autonomous Growth Brain

## Purpose
Turn analytics and audience signals into a constrained daily content decision without allowing engagement goals to override biblical accuracy, Gospel clarity, safety, honesty, or platform compliance.

## Inputs
- Recent performance records
- Saved AI growth strategy
- Audience profile (aggregate public signals only)
- Topic recency
- Active content experiments

## Outputs
- Topic
- Content pillar
- Hook direction
- Format
- Experiment context
- Confidence level
- Evidence summary

## Guardrails
- Sparse data is explicitly marked LOW confidence.
- Recent-topic repetition is avoided where possible.
- The decision layer cannot bypass the biblical quality gate.
- No follower-count or virality guarantees.
- Daily autopilot stops if the brain response is missing or invalid.

## Daily sequence
1. Audience/analytics jobs update signals.
2. Growth strategy is refreshed.
3. Growth Brain selects the next content direction.
4. Generator receives the decision.
5. Biblical/safety quality gate must PASS.
6. Renderer produces the short.
7. Platform validation must PASS.
8. Metricool schedules the approved asset.
9. Performance returns to the learning loop.
