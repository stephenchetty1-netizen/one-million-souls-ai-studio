# V42 — Autonomous Biblical Source Hierarchy & Evidence Engine

V42 establishes an explicit evidence hierarchy for biblical content. Primary Scripture is authoritative; strong reference material can support context; secondary commentary is informative but cannot establish biblical claims by itself; unverified material cannot be used as evidence.

## Status
- PASS: claims have verified primary support.
- REVIEW: evidence is verified but primary support is incomplete.
- BLOCK: material unsupported/rejected claims or evidence exist.

## Guardrails
- Never invent citations or sources.
- Never let commentary outrank Scripture.
- Never bypass theological, safety, rights, or final quality gates.
- Use public evidence only.
