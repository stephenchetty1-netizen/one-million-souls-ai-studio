# OMS Premium YouTube Single-Prompt Build

Build a 59-second vertical Christian YouTube Short for One Million Souls about **Matthew 5:16 — Let Your Light Shine**.

Use 1080x1920 at 30fps. The result must look and sound premium. This is a hard requirement, not a preference.

## Premium-only media rules

- Background tier: **PREMIUM_CINEMATIC**
- Graphics tier: **PREMIUM_MOTION**
- Voice tier: **PREMIUM_NEURAL**
- Never use plain placeholder gradients as the finished visual treatment.
- Use layered cinematic lighting, volumetric glow, depth, parallax, glass/HUD treatment, fine particles, light sweeps, premium typography and controlled motion.
- No stock-looking generic templates.
- No low-resolution or visibly AI-broken imagery.
- No robotic TTS, espeak, novelty voices, or low-quality fallback speech.
- Use a natural premium neural narration voice only.
- If the premium voice cannot be generated, fail the render instead of producing a silent master.
- The final MP4 must contain a verified audible AAC narration stream.
- Captions must follow the narration.
- Keep publication locked until normal OMS review/certification passes.

## 8-scene structure

1. 0-4s — hook: **STOP HIDING YOUR LIGHT**
2. 4-10s — fear vs faith contrast
3. 10-18s — Scripture reveal: Matthew 5:16
4. 18-27s — kindness, courage, truth
5. 27-36s — pray, serve, speak life, show grace
6. 36-45s — practical faith steps
7. 45-54s — one light encouraging another
8. 54-59s — One Million Souls CTA

Motion must feel deliberate and editorial: spring entrances, staggered reveals, parallax depth, cinematic light movement, polished cards, safe-zone typography, word-highlight captions, and a strong final brand payoff.
