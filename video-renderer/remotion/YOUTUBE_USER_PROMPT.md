# OMS YouTube Single-Prompt Build

Use this as the one-shot user prompt for the `OMS-YouTube-Single-Prompt` Remotion composition.

Build a 59-second vertical Christian YouTube Short for One Million Souls about **Matthew 5:16 — Let Your Light Shine**.

Use 1080x1920 at 30fps. Make it feel like a premium cinematic launch video rather than a static devotional slideshow.

Create exactly 8 scenes:
1. 0-4s — hook: **STOP HIDING YOUR LIGHT**
2. 4-10s — fear vs faith contrast
3. 10-18s — Scripture reveal: Matthew 5:16
4. 18-27s — three visible faith pillars: kindness, courage, truth
5. 27-36s — action chips: pray, serve, speak life, show grace
6. 36-45s — three practical steps for today
7. 45-54s — network/orbit visual showing one light encouraging another
8. 54-59s — strong One Million Souls CTA

Motion language:
- spring-based entrances
- staggered reveals
- subtle moving grid
- particles and scanner lines
- glass/HUD panels
- high-contrast typography
- purposeful motion only; no random bouncing
- strong visual change every few seconds
- mobile safe zones
- word-level highlighted captions

Brand:
- dark navy / near-black
- electric blue
- gold accent
- white / silver typography
- ONE MILLION SOULS footer identity

Content rules:
- Scripture stays central and accurately referenced.
- Do not turn the verse into prosperity promises or manipulative claims.
- No misleading clickbait.
- No unlicensed third-party media.
- Keep publication locked until normal OMS review/certification passes.
